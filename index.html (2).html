Understand your solar before you buy it.

Plan your rooftop solar, step by step.

Size your system, see it on your roof, get a parts list, check the subsidy, compare installer quotes, and finish with a Solar Project Passport you can take to any installer. Free, and your entries stay on this device.

    Your location and electricity use

    Start with your state and your monthly units. This alone gives you a first estimate.

    Energy charges ÷ units billed. Fixed charges aren't reduced by solar.
    Don't know your units? Estimate from appliances

    Your roof

    Measure the open, shade-free part of your roof. A tape measure and a few minutes is enough.

    Check in winter if you can: the sun is lower and shadows are longest.

    Your system

    The recommended size, how much it will produce each month, and what it saves you.

    Equipment and checks

    A parts list for your system, with electrical checks that panels and inverter work together.

    Panel details

    Defaults are a typical 540 W panel. Copy values from the datasheet of the panel you're offered.
    Inverter details (optional)
    Leave empty to use typical values for the recommended size. Fill in from the inverter datasheet once you have a model.

    Subsidy and approvals

    What you can get from PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana, and the steps to get it. Tick each one as you finish it.

    Before you apply

      Application steps

        Apply at pmsuryaghar.gov.in. Some states pay an extra subsidy; ask your DISCOM.

        Check an installer's quote

        Enter up to two quotes. We compare each against your own system and flag what's missing or unusual.

        Your Solar Project Passport

        Everything about your project in one place. Show it to installers so every quote is for the same system.
